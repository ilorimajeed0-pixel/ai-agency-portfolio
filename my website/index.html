How I work

Four steps from first call to launch

Scope, risk and expected results are agreed before anything goes live.

  1. Review your current process

    I map your calls, data handoffs and manual steps, and note where time and leads are lost. You get a clear scope before any build starts.

  2. Design and test

    Conversation flows, automations and escalation paths are built around your tools, then tested against real scenarios and edge cases.

  3. Launch alongside your current process

    The new system runs in parallel before switching over, so your phones, forms and CRM keep working throughout.

  4. Monitor and improve

    I review call quality, workflow runs and error logs after launch. Ongoing support terms are agreed in writing for each project.

Featured work

Autonomous Cold Outreach & Intent-Based Reply Processing Engine

Two n8n workflows that send a three-stage follow-up sequence and sort every reply by intent.

  • Follow-ups: a scheduled run reads the lead sheet, and Google Gemini drafts follow-up 1, 2 or 3 for each lead that’s due.
  • Replies: each new reply is matched to its lead and classified as Interested, Not Interested, Do Not Contact or Other.
  • Actions: the lead’s status is updated in the sheet and the right response is sent automatically.

Responsible AI

Built so you stay in control

Human handoff

Defined triggers — specific requests, keywords or questions the agent can’t answer — transfer the caller to your team, and every handoff is logged.

Clear scope

Agents work from your approved scripts. Anything outside them is declined or passed to a person, so the system can’t make pricing or contractual promises for you.

Privacy and disclosure

Data travels over encrypted connections and is kept only as long as each workflow needs. Calls can open with an AI and recording disclosure set up for your jurisdiction.

⚠

Scope notice: these systems handle administrative work — intake, qualification, routing, notifications and data sync. They don’t give legal, financial or clinical advice; those requests are always passed to qualified staff.

FAQ

Common questions

What is an AI voice agent?

A phone assistant powered by AI that answers calls, responds to common questions, asks qualifying questions and routes callers to the right person. I build them on Retell AI.

Can the voice agent book appointments?

Not yet. Booking directly by voice is on the roadmap. Today the agent answers inbound and outbound calls, handles FAQs, qualifies leads and transfers callers to your team to book.

Which tools do you use?

Retell AI for voice agents, and n8n or Make.com for workflow automation, connected to the tools you already use such as Google Sheets, Gmail, Slack and your CRM. The choice depends on your requirements.

Which industries do you work with?

Healthcare and dental, real estate, home services, e-commerce, professional services, B2B and SaaS, hospitality and logistics — any business that handles a steady flow of calls and inquiries.

Where do you work?

Worldwide. All work is done remotely, so your location and time zone aren’t a barrier.
