About Me

I build AI-powered voice agents and workflow automations designed to help businesses manage communication and repetitive tasks more efficiently. My work includes Retell AI voice-agent development, n8n workflow automation, and Make.com integrations.

My focus is on practical systems that answer customer conversations, capture leads, and connect business tools — for healthcare and dental practices, real estate agencies, home service companies, e-commerce stores, B2B teams, and more.

I work with clients across Australia and global English-speaking markets. The aim is always the same: take repetitive calls, messages, and data entry off your team's plate, with a clear path to a human whenever it matters.
