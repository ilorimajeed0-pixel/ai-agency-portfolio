Every AI system I build prioritises customer safety, transparency, and appropriate scope for its industry.

Sensitive, urgent, or complex inquiries are escalated to qualified members of your team. AI handles only routine, administrative interactions within clearly defined boundaries.

ALWAYS ACTIVE
