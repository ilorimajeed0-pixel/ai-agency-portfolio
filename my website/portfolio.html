Featured case study · n8n · Google Gemini

Autonomous Cold Outreach & Intent-Based Reply Processing Engine

Two n8n workflows share one Google Sheets lead register. Cold Outreach – Follow Ups runs a three-stage follow-up sequence on a schedule. Cold Outreach – Reply Detection reads each reply, classifies its intent with Google Gemini, updates the lead and sends the matching response, with no one sorting the inbox by hand.

The problem it solves

Slow reply handling. In a manual outbound process, replies wait in the inbox until someone reads and acts on each one.

Missed follow-ups. Interested prospects go cold when follow-ups depend on someone remembering which lead is due.

Out-of-date records. Lead status is re-typed by hand, so records drift — and opt-outs risk being emailed again.

How it’s built

Workflow A

Follow-up sequence

  1. Schedule trigger starts a batch run.
  2. Google Sheets reads the lead register.
  3. Switch routes each lead by stage — follow-up 1, 2, 3 or a final branch — and a filter passes only leads that are due.
  4. AI agent with Gemini drafts the follow-up for that stage.
  5. Gmail sends it and Sheets records the new stage.
  6. The final branch updates the row without sending, closing the sequence.

Workflow B

Reply detection and intent sorting

  1. Gmail trigger picks up each new reply when it checks the inbox.
  2. Filter skips messages that aren’t outreach replies.
  3. Google Sheets looks up the sender; a second filter continues only for known leads.
  4. AI agent with Gemini classifies the reply’s intent.
  5. Switch branches four ways: Interested, Not Interested, Do Not Contact, Other.
  6. Each branch updates the lead’s status, then Gmail sends the right response.

Impact

What the design guarantees, and how each point is measured.

Reply classificationTarget: under 10 seconds

Gemini classification and routing, timed per run in n8n’s execution log. End-to-end time also includes the Gmail polling interval.

Manual re-typing100% of status updates automated

Every follow-up sent and every classified reply updates the lead row automatically.

Dropped leadsNo silent-drop path

Every reply from a known lead lands in a branch; anything unclear goes to “Other” instead of being discarded.

Opt-outsRecorded on receipt

“Do Not Contact” replies are written to the same register the follow-up sequence reads.

⚠

Limits:

  • The Gmail trigger checks the inbox on a timer rather than receiving instant notifications, so reply handling takes up to one polling interval plus model time.
  • Classification quality depends on the prompt and model; unclear replies go to “Other” for a person to review.
  • The lead register is a Google Sheet. Syncing with a CRM such as HubSpot would be a separate integration.

Client results such as reply rates, hours saved or meetings booked will be published once measured over a full campaign. None are claimed here.
