About me

I build AI voice agents and workflow automations that help businesses handle calls, messages and repetitive tasks more efficiently. My work includes Retell AI voice-agent development, n8n workflow automation and Make.com integrations.

I focus on practical systems that answer customer conversations, capture leads and connect business tools — for healthcare and dental practices, real estate agencies, home service companies, e-commerce stores, B2B teams and more.

I work with clients worldwide, entirely remotely. The aim is always the same: take repetitive calls, messages and data entry off your team’s plate, with a clear path to a human whenever it matters.
