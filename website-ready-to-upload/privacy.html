Privacy notice

Last updated: 23 October 2026

Who I am

This website belongs to Ilori Majeed, an independent AI voice agent and automation specialist. If you have any question about your information, email ilorimajeed0@gmail.com.

What I collect

The site only collects what you choose to send through an inquiry form:

  • your name and email address
  • the subject, your business type, the service you’re interested in, and your message
  • optionally, how you heard about me

Please don’t include sensitive personal data, such as patient records or payment details, in an inquiry.

How I use it

I use your details only to read and reply to your inquiry and, if we work together, to discuss the project. I don’t sell your information or add you to a mailing list.

Services that handle your data

  • Web3Forms receives what you submit through a form and delivers it to my email inbox.
  • My email provider stores the inquiry once it arrives in my inbox.
  • Google Fonts supplies the site’s fonts. Your browser requests them from Google’s servers, which receive your IP address as part of that request.
  • The hosting provider that serves this website may keep standard technical logs, such as IP addresses and the pages requested.
  • WhatsApp and email links open those apps; anything you send there is handled under their own privacy terms.

Cookies and storage

This site doesn’t use cookies, analytics or advertising trackers. When you send a form, your browser stores the time of that submission locally so it can block duplicate messages for 60 seconds. It never leaves your device.

How long I keep it

Inquiries stay in my email for as long as they’re needed to handle your request and any work that follows. You can ask me to delete them at any time.

Your choices

You can ask to see, correct or delete the information you sent me by emailing ilorimajeed0@gmail.com. I’ll respond as soon as I can.

Changes

If this notice changes, the date at the top of the page will be updated.
