01 · Flagship

AI Voice Agents

AI receptionists and phone agents for inbound and outbound calls, built on Retell AI.

I build voice agents that act as receptionists for businesses — clinics, real estate agencies, home service companies, online stores and more. They answer common questions, ask qualifying questions, route calls to the right person or department, and work 24/7, always with a path to a human.

Live now

  • Inbound call handling (customer FAQs and general inquiries)
  • Lead qualification questions
  • Outbound calling campaigns
  • Transfers to people or departments
  • Answers drawn from your approved content
  • Multi-turn conversations
  • Call logging for every session

On the roadmap — not yet live

  • Booking appointments directly by voice
  • Live appointment rescheduling
  • Live CRM or calendar updates during a call
  • Direct integration with industry systems such as EHR/PMS or property CRMs

Until these ship, qualified callers are transferred to your team to book or reschedule.

Typical uses

  • After-hours receptionist
  • Real estate lead qualification
  • Home services job inquiries
  • Clinic and dental FAQ calls
  • Outbound follow-up campaigns

Workflow Automation

n8n

Multi-step workflows

Workflows that connect form submissions, CRMs, email, Slack and other tools — with data checks, error handling and routing to more than one destination.

  • Lead and customer inquiry intake
  • CRM sync and contact management
  • Slack and email notifications
  • Webhook triggers
  • Conditional logic and error handling
  • Multi-step data transformation

Typical flow

  1. Trigger — a form, call or webhook arrives
  2. Check — required fields and email format
  3. Route — by inquiry type
  4. Update — create or update the CRM record
  5. Notify — alert the team

Responsible AI

My commitment

Every system I build puts customer safety, transparency and an appropriate scope first.

Human handoff

Sensitive, urgent or complex inquiries go to qualified members of your team. The AI handles only routine administrative conversations within clear limits.

Administrative work only

Systems handle administrative and communication tasks. For healthcare clients, no system gives clinical diagnosis, medical advice, treatment recommendations or prescriptions.

Careful data handling

Systems collect only the data each workflow needs, and you keep full ownership of your data at every step.
